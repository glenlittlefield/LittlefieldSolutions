FAQ

Common Questions

Helpful answers before you reach out about a new website or redesign.

How long does a website take?

Most small business websites can be completed quickly once content, business information, and design direction are provided. Timeline depends on package size and revision needs.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Existing websites can be modernized with a cleaner layout, better mobile experience, clearer calls to action, and improved service presentation.

Do you provide hosting?

The site is built as a static website that can be hosted on GitHub Pages or another static hosting provider. Guidance can be provided for getting it online.

Can I request revisions?

Yes. Each project includes review time so the final website matches your business and communicates the right message to customers.

What payment methods do you accept?

PayPal, Cash App, and Venmo are accepted. Payment details are shared after project scope and terms are agreed upon.
